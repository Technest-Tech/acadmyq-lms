A Academiq · Super Admin · Redesign proposal

Modules belong to the client, not to the sidebar

One page per job, one place to change each fact. You pick Management / Video / WhatsApp per client with three switches, the free trial has one clock per module, and nothing is duplicated. All screens below are mockups with sample data.

1 Why it feels broken

The panel shows three old systems at once

Your instinct is right. The new per-module subscription model exists in the database and works (the access engine already uses it) — but every admin page still reads and writes the old tables. Management billing, video access, and WhatsApp settings are three separate systems, each with its own buttons on different pages. That is the conflict you feel.

Other findings: the academy page still shows the dead Wasender token panel next to the real QR connect; creating an academy cannot set modules or trial length (a trial only happens through the FREE-plan trick, and video/WhatsApp must be configured later on two other pages); and the plan editor cannot create Video or WhatsApp plans at all.

2 The new track

A short flat sidebar — the “module” idea moves into the client

Two rules drive everything:

1 · Client-first. You manage clients, and each client has up to three subscriptions (Management, Video, WhatsApp). Any mix, each with its own plan, its own trial, its own price.

2 · One writer per fact. Every fact can be changed on exactly one page. Everywhere else it is read-only and links there. This is what removes the conflicts permanently.

4 The anti-conflict rule

One writer per fact

This table becomes law for the panel. Anything not on its page shows as read-only with a link. This is what guarantees “no duplicates” stays true next year, not just after this redesign.

6 Build order

Five phases — your biggest pains fixed in the first two

Make per-module subscriptions the real engine (backend)

Trial / activate / pause / prices move onto the per-module rows; one expiry job; per-module plan editing; new client endpoints. Old tables stay as mirrors until R5.

You get: one trial clock per module that always reflects correctly — the “5 places for trial” problem dies here.

The Clients hub (frontend)

New roster with module chips, the client page with the Subscriptions card and tabs, the new wizard, the flat sidebar. Old routes redirect.

You get: “management only / video only / WhatsApp only / any mix” as three switches, and one page per client.

Billing + Plans rebuild

Billing becomes money-only (proof inbox, dues, MRR per module). Plans get module tabs and the unhidden add-on editor.

You get: subscriptions and packages readable at a glance.

Ops cleanup + WhatsApp-only clients

Video Ops and WhatsApp Ops keep only platform-wide monitoring; per-client controls finish moving to client tabs; legacy panels deleted; the wizard’s no-login WhatsApp-only path ships.

You get: zero duplicated controls anywhere.

Drop the legacy tables

Old plan/video columns and the old subscription table are removed once nothing reads them. The mirrors and redirects go away.

You get: one system, permanently — no way for old and new to disagree again.
To approve this direction, four small questions (defaults in brackets apply if you just say “go”):
  1. Default trial length per module? [5 days for all three]
  2. When you create a client on a paid plan, start with a trial anyway? [yes — you can click “activate paid now” instead]
  3. Staff Departments moves into Settings? [yes]
  4. Payment-proof review stays one global inbox on Billing? [yes]
